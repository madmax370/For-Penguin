29 July 💜
Dear Bhandhari!

Assalamualaikum.

Thank you so much tune mere liye jitna kuch bhi kiya hai uske liye. Aur sorry agar maine tujhe kabhi dukh pahunchaya ho toh.

Dekh, tu baat nahi kar sakti, chalenga. Maine tujhse dosti isliye nahi ki thi ke tu roz mujhse baat kare ya mujhe bataye ke tu kya kar rahi hai. Mere liye woh sab matter nahi karta. Mere liye sabse zyada teri khushi aur teri safety matter karti hai. Mujhe pata hai tu pehle hi kitni mushkilon ke saath baat karti hai. Main teri mushkile aur nahi badhaunga.

Bas ek baat ka dukh rehta hai. Jab bhi koi problem hoti hai, tu hamesha sab khatam karne ki baat kyun karti hai? Itni jaldi sab khatam ho jaata hai kya? Mujhe woh baat samajh nahi aati. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai, tu chahti hai main teri life se chala jaaun. Khair, jaane de.

Hamari baat na ho, iska matlab ye toh nahi na ke hamari dosti khatam ho gayi? Ya phir tu mere liye important nahi rahi? Mere liye tu pehle bhi meri sabse important person thi aur hamesha rahengi, chahe main teri life mein rahun ya na rahun.

Bas tu khush reh, apni studies par dhyan de. Main hamesha dil se tere saath rahunga. Aur jo project app main bana raha hu na, woh khaas tere liye hi bana raha hu. Mujhe umeed hai woh teri studies mein help karega, phir tujhe kisi ki help ki zarurat nahi padegi.

Please, I am really sorry. Tu mujhse naraz mat reh. Teri narazgi sach mein mujhe bahut takleef deti hai.

Aur last mein, tu mujhe yaad kare ya na kare. 😅 Kabhi meri yaad aa jaaye toh AUR ke ye do songs zaroor sunna.

Kabhi Kabhi
You

Jab bhi main tujhe miss karta hu na, main yehi dono songs sunta hu.

Chal, bye. Apna khayal rakh. Aur kabhi bahar milu na, toh saamne se ek baar awaaz de dena yaar. Mujhme itni himmat hi nahi hoti tujhe pehle awaaz dene ki.

Aur main window ki taraf bhi isliye nahi dekhta, kyunki dar lagta hai. Agar main dekhu aur tere badle koi aur wahan khada ho, toh pata nahi kaisa lagega. Isliye main dekhna hi avoid karta hu. Main kabhi nahi chahta ke meri wajah se teri life mein koi problem aaye.

Aur kal Isha ke time mujhe laga maine teri awaaz suni. Shayad tu keh rahi thi, "Mumma, yahan nahi hai." Shayad tu apni Ammi se baat kar rahi thi. Main galat bhi ho sakta hu.

Bye, take care Bhandhari. 💜

~ Bhatari 🤍
